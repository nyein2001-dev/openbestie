## Decisions made

- **Assumption — customer.** Sell first to independent short-form video creators who have already tried carrying one character across episodes and can choose a new, nonexclusive cast member.
- **Assumption — offer.** Launch three original, stylized nonhuman characters. Show each across two different shots before asking for payment. Start with one commercially cleared, tested Kling workflow.
- **Assumption — business.** One Thailand-based individual creates the files offline and runs the shop. Sell individual ZIPs directly through Gumroad at $19 for the first ten packs collectively, then $29.
- **Estimate — December goal.** Reach 500 measured visitors, ten distinct paying customers and at least $190 in gross product sales. Aim to earn the first sale in the build-week pilot. Close the target by December 30, Bangkok time, and reconcile on December 31.

# Open Bestie | Business overview

**Assumption — value proposition.** “Cast a character for your next two shots. See the same character in two scenes, then download the references and prompts used to make them.” A buyer can inspect identity, silhouette and markings before risking a paid generation. The seller promises the specified inputs and shows one observed result. The buyer supplies a compatible tool account and generation credits.

**Fact — why now.** Kling documents a workflow that binds a subject from two to four reference images. A creator independently describes reusing the same references across shots. These are evidence of a workable input format and a real task. Neither measures demand for this store. [Kling model guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide) · [Creator workflow](https://www.reddit.com/r/aivideo/comments/1s3hg5n/keeping_2_characters_consistent_across_ai_video/) (accessed 2026-11-26).

**Fact — the buyer has a free alternative.** Turnaround Sheet advertises free character references and example prompts. ODDLIES lists an original character pack at €34.99 with clips and a license. A listing price measures the seller's ask. No verified sell-through for a comparable nonhuman pack at our price was found. That makes the paid offer in [02](02-problem-validation.md) the first commercial milestone. [Turnaround Sheet](https://turnaroundsheet.com/) · [ODDLIES terms and prices](https://oddlies.shop/en/terms) (accessed 2026-11-26).

## Assumptions & open questions

- Does the founder have editable, rights-cleared masters close enough to completion to prepare three characters in the allotted hours? If only one clears the test, sell that one.
- Will a creator use someone else's nonexclusive character when free assets or their existing cast are available? Test with money and a second shot produced without coaching.
- Will Gumroad verify this individual's account and release the first THB deposit in December? The founder's payout screen and bank statement settle that question.
