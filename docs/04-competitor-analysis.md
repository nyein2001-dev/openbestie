## Decisions made

- **Assumption.** Position Open Bestie as a small cast of original nonhuman characters with reproducible, dated two-shot demonstrations, clear source files and one nonexclusive commercial-use license.
- **Assumption.** Put an actual cast member, its price and its footage in the first screen. Let visitors choose a character before asking them to pay or identify themselves.
- **Assumption.** Reject a volume-of-files contest with low-priced stock and a bespoke commissioning offer. The former has a strong free substitute and the latter consumes founder time per sale.
- **Assumption.** Use hosted guest checkout and receipt-based recovery. A custom cart, storefront account and “adoption” vocabulary add steps to a one-character purchase.

### Four homepage teardowns

**Fact** describes visible hierarchy and the path offered to the visitor. **Assumption** describes how to apply it to Open Bestie. These are design observations with no measured conversion uplift.

| Homepage | First-screen promise → evaluation → next action, fact | Adopt or reject, assumption |
|---|---|---|
| [ODDLIES](https://oddlies.shop/en/) | Group portrait and stylized headline introduce a memorable cast. Tool references and “Explore templates” lead through character features and priced originals to cart. Pack contents and usage grants appear farther down. | Feature one purchasable original immediately. Keep character personality in the image and name. Reject fan art, illustrative “demo views” and availability theater as substitutes for customer proof. |
| [Turnaround Sheet](https://turnaroundsheet.com/) | Hero names the deliverable, invites a search by profession and offers free packs. A featured character pairs artwork, full body and turnaround. Worked ads connect outputs to specific packs and prompts. | Pair the hero’s input with two outputs and link directly to that pack. Show the other characters just below. Three items do not justify the site's role search, genre tree or extensive filters. |
| [CharaFold](https://charafold.com/) | A short headline, a reference portrait and labeled output views sit beside Create and See examples. A recorded first-pass test reveals a framing miss. The page gives a one-time price and a step-by-step process. | Label sample media with its actual method and disclose a known failure mode. Show the pack price up front. The CTA opens the character for sale rather than a generation form. |
| [aicastshare](https://aicastshare.com/) | Released packs are differentiated from preview-only assets. Licensing explains credit, modification and redistribution. Its browsing guide leads through character, license and download. | Mark what the paid ZIP contains versus what is shown for illustration, and summarize finished-video rights at the buy control. Omit unreleased teaser cards from a small paid catalog. |

**Assumption — resulting path.** On mobile, let the visitor recognize the character in the first shot, start the second on demand, inspect source thumbnails, check price and rights, then move to guest payment. Use “Buy character pack” and “Purchases & downloads.” Existing buyers recover files from the purchase receipt or native library. Gumroad documents purchase without an account, an emailed receipt link and optional library access. [Gumroad customer flow](https://gumroad.com/help/article/282-how-do-purchases-work-for-my-customers) · [Home, detail and admin requirements](08-product-requirements.md)

**Assumption — differentiated positioning.** “Cast a character for your next short. See the same character in two shots, then download the references and steps used to make them.” A competitor already publishes worked examples, and another already sells characters with clips. The claim to prove is that a buyer prefers this cast and reproduces a shot from the delivered files. No category, feature or global exclusivity claim is needed.

## Assumptions & open questions

- Does seeing the same nonhuman character from two angles change a purchase decision at $19? Ask declining prospects to show their chosen alternative.
- Will customers with an established cast ever take a nonexclusive guest character? Record actual episode use before widening targeting.
- Which blocker appears at $29 after the global first ten packs sell: price, appearance, plan access or freedom to use the finished video? Code the buyer’s own explanation.
