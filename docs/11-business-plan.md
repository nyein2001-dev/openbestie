## Decisions made

- **Assumption.** Launch Open Bestie on Dec 10 as a single-seller digital character shop for independent creators casting recurring shorts.
- **Assumption.** Sell one original nonhuman character per purchase. Demonstrate it in two shots in the tested Kling workflow and show the actual inputs, price and commercial rights.
- **Estimate.** Target 500 measured visitors, ten retained paying customers and $190 gross by Dec 30. Close the calendar-month accounts on Dec 31. Record Thai bank settlement separately.
- **Assumption.** Authorize at most $69 in startup and December operating spend, before per-order deductions. Increase investment only when actual paid decisions support it.

# Open Bestie | Business plan

### One-page lean canvas · November 26, 2026

The canvas uses Ash Maurya's nine business-model questions. Commercial choices below are **assumptions** unless labeled otherwise. [Lean Canvas method](https://ashmaurya.com/blog/what-is-lean-canvas) (accessed 2026-11-26).

| Canvas block | Launch answer |
|---|---|
| **Customer segments** | One early buyer is a short-form creator with a recurring series, an upcoming role, an eligible video-tool account and the freedom to cast a nonexclusive character. |
| **Problem** | Creating references takes effort, and identity can drift between shots. The question that matters for sales is whether the creator wants one of these particular characters. **Fact.** A creator reported reusing reference images across a real edit. A free library already offers character references. [Creator workflow](https://www.reddit.com/r/aivideo/comments/1s3hg5n/keeping_2_characters_consistent_across_ai_video/) · [Free alternative](https://turnaroundsheet.com/) (accessed 2026-11-26). |
| **Unique value proposition** | Cast the character after seeing it perform. The buyer can compare the source reference with two actual shots and download the inputs that produced them. |
| **Solution** | Three original cast members. Each ZIP contains three separate PNG views, two copyable prompts, quick-start and one commercial license. Show a dated two-shot example per pack on the storefront. |
| **Channels** | Small tutorial partners, direct opt-in conversations, permitted posts in existing creator communities, and owned demonstrations. Spend up to 24 acquisition hours across these four. |
| **Revenue streams** | One-time, direct sales of licensed character packs. $29 standard. A single opening discount makes the first ten pack units across the shop $19. One purchaser can commercially distribute finished videos. |
| **Cost structure** | **Estimate.** $69 total startup and December outlay, then $24/month in Q1. Model direct card fees as 12.9% + $0.80/order and reserve 5% of gross for risk. Founder time is valued separately. [Gumroad fees](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) (accessed 2026-11-26). |
| **Key metrics** | Visitors → character detail → checkout click → paying buyer. Count usable shots among buyers who tried the pack, refunds, contribution per hour and THB credited. The native order ledger determines paid sales. |
| **Unfair advantage** | No durable advantage has been demonstrated. Candidate advantage is the founder's ability to create distinctive, rights-cleared cast members and personally reproduce buyers' shots. Test whether buyers choose this cast over free work. |

### Decisions the founder can act on

| Question | Decision and evidence |
|---|---|
| Is the problem strong enough to sell against? | **Fact.** A paid direct competitor lists €34.99 for a character, but public pricing and a sold-out label cannot establish paid volume. **Assumption.** Retain the offer only if qualified people pay. Seek two independent buyers in ten offers for the first finished pack and two successful reproductions from three testers. [ODDLIES terms](https://oddlies.shop/en/terms) (accessed 2026-11-26) · [Validation](02-problem-validation.md) · [Alternatives](04-competitor-analysis.md). |
| What must ship? | **Estimate.** One founder, 14 calendar days, 98 hours total. Build the home, product pages and minimal owner panel. Integrate guest checkout, delivery, orders and refunds. The first purchase path must work on a phone, including receipt recovery. [Scope and capacity](07-mvp-feature-prioritization.md) · [Acceptance tests](08-product-requirements.md). |
| Can the individual sell and receive money? | **Fact.** Gumroad lists Thai local bank payouts and handles buyer indirect tax as reseller on covered transactions. Review and settlement can delay a first deposit. **Assumption.** Confirm the actual seller, bank and SKU before collecting money. Require documented source rights and paid Kling commercial terms. Offer 14-day refunds. [Gumroad payouts](https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid) · [Gumroad tax](https://gumroad.com/help/article/121-sales-tax-on-gumroad) · [Kling paid terms](https://kling.ai/docs/payment-policy) (accessed 2026-11-26) · [Commercial model](05-business-model.md) · [Legal gates](10-risk-and-legal-assessment.md). |
| What does success buy? | **Estimate.** December base receipts of $190 leave $157.49 before the reserve and fixed spending and $78.99 after both. Base Q1, using the same catalog, is 36 buyers, $1,044 gross and $756.32 contribution after allowance and planned overhead. Labor, income tax and settlement timing matter before declaring a profitable livelihood. [Financial model](06-revenue-and-financial-plan.md). |
| Why this narrow offer? | **Assumption.** Three visible characters, one workflow and one license let a stranger decide unaided. A broad genre catalog adds unvalidated inventory. A commission service ties every sale to new production. A subscription requires evidence of repeat demand. [Buyer segment and 90-day scope](03-market-research.md) · [Founder hypotheses assessed](01-business-overview.md). |
| When should the founder change direction? | **Assumption.** When reach is inadequate, change distribution. When checkout fails, fix it immediately. When two qualified cohorts each yield zero paid orders through working checkout and creators need their existing casts, stop investing in this offer. Other feature requests must pass the evidence and cost rules in [07](07-mvp-feature-prioritization.md). [Channel plan](09-go-to-market-strategy.md) · [Operating calendar](12-launch-roadmap.md). |

### Reconciliation across the plan

**Estimate.** The four channels in [09](09-go-to-market-strategy.md) sum to 500 December visitors and ten customers. Two assumed prelaunch buyers use two discounted units. The first 90 public days therefore yield about 34 buyers and $906 gross in [03](03-market-research.md). Q1 counts Jan 1–Mar 31 and has 36 buyers at $29 in the base case. The ten discounts run once across all three packs. The daily allocations in [12](12-launch-roadmap.md) sum to the 60 engineering, 24 founder and 14 buffer hours in [07](07-mvp-feature-prioritization.md).

## Assumptions & open questions

- Do enough creators actively need a new character they can share nonexclusively with other licensees? Free alternatives make payment the critical evidence.
- Can the founder clear source rights, reproduce each workflow and receive genuine orders early enough for a December bank credit?
- Will buyers still choose a character at $29 after the ten discounted units are used, and will the best channel cover its founder time?
