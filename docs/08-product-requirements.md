## Decisions made

- **Assumption —** Lead with the same character in two different shots. Show the actual input, workflow and price before asking for payment. Each of three launch packs has its own shareable detail page.
- **Assumption —** Use “Browse characters,” “View pack,” “Buy character pack” and “Purchases & downloads” as the navigation and action labels. Make the home page the entire catalog.
- **Assumption —** Send buyers directly to Gumroad as guests. Give them access from the confirmation and receipt, with an optional provider library. Gumroad owns the transaction and files.
- **Assumption —** Give the owner a panel for catalog editing, order resolution and feedback. Build the catalog and feedback screens. Link directly to the hosted sales tools.

### What informed the interface

**Fact.** The following are observations about public interfaces and first-party research. None publishes an Open Bestie conversion rate. The right column contains **assumptions** to test with five creator walkthroughs and paid orders.

| Observation | Decision and rejected alternative |
| --- | --- |
| Fab's home features individual assets within browsable groups. Its listing guidance puts a media gallery, license, price and file formats together. Its purchase flow offers Buy now and a library. [Fab home](https://www.fab.com/) · [Fab purchase guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fab/purchasing-and-downloading-assets-in-fab) (accessed 2026-11-26). | Put a face, role, workflow and price on each card. Put the license and exact contents beside Buy. Three visible cards make a category directory or search bar unnecessary. |

### Home page specification

**Assumption — wireframe and copy.** At 390 px use one column. At desktop the hero is copy beside proof, then three cards in one row. Keep the hero text, primary CTA and useful still visible without starting a video. Check the fold at 390 × 844, 360 × 640 and 1440 × 900. On shorter screens prioritize headline and action, followed immediately by proof.

| Order | Section and content | Interaction and acceptance |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Header.** Wordmark links home. “Characters” jumps to the catalog. “Purchases & downloads” stays visible. | At 320 px, links wrap or shorten to “Downloads” with the same accessible name. No account gate, horizontal scrolling or navigation drawer for two links. |
| 2 | **Featured character.** H1 “Cast a character for your next AI video.” Follow with “Download the references and prompts used to make these two shots.” Use a still from each shot of the same published character, with its name and actual tested workflow. | Main CTA “View [name] pack” deep-links to the featured SKU. Secondary text link “Browse all characters.” Show “USD 29 per pack. First 10 packs USD 19 at checkout, while available.” After exhaustion display only the standard price. No countdown. |
| 3 | **Evidence within the hero.** The stills visibly differ in setting or framing. Label them “Shot 1” and “Shot 2.” A play control opens or plays their short, silent sequence inline. Give names for each setting and a text description of visible drift. | Poster renders without JS or a video request. Play/pause/seek are keyboard-operable. Tool, model/mode, plan and test date come from the featured pack. When the clip fails to load, the two stills and detail link still work. |
| 4 | **Characters.** Show every published pack once, including the featured one. Use full subject framing, character name, one role/use case, tested tool, USD price/offer terms and “View pack.” Frame artwork consistently to support visual comparison. | One card link with a descriptive accessible name. No hover-only details. Sort by owner's fixed display order. Show no empty slots, unearned “bestseller” labels or invented reviews. |
| 5 | **Inside a pack.** Show a reduced sample of one actual reference and explain how to unzip it, upload the indicated reference in the stated tool/mode and generate with a supplied prompt. List three PNGs, two prompts, quick-start and license. | Link to the featured pack's exact dimensions and workflow notes. Label publicly viewable proof separately from delivered files. Clearly state the buyer supplies their own tool access and generation credits. |
| 6 | **License, risk and support.** Explain nonexclusive use of finished videos, source-file resale restriction, tool-plan requirements, results variability and 14-calendar-day money-back policy. State who made the pack and who processes the payment. | Link to full license/refund terms and contact. Repeat “Browse characters” after objections. Footer links to purchases, privacy, license, refunds and contact. Use a real named contact or monitored address. |

**Estimate — test method.** Use five of the eight interviewees in [02](02-problem-validation.md) for short, individual home-page tasks after their interviews. Before explaining it, ask them to choose a recurring character, say what arrives after payment, identify the tested tool and find the refund terms. Record the point of confusion. Four of five completing those tasks unaided is the usability target. Fix repeated blockers and rerun the task. Compare actual visitor → detail → checkout-click → paid counts in [12](12-launch-roadmap.md).

**Assumption — workflow proof.** Use paid Kling Standard as the first test route subject to rights verification. ### Visual design and nonfunctional release gates

**Assumption — visual direction.** Use ink navy (#101522) behind neutral ivory text (#F4F5F2), blue-grey supporting text (#B7C3D5) and a single mint action color (#70E9CC) with ink labels. Thin cool-grey dividers separate sections. Character stills provide depth through light and shadow already present in the artwork. Limit glow to a soft border around the featured still. Keep text and checkout controls solid. Use locally served **Geist** for headings and body with a system sans fallback. Set body text to 16 px or larger, a 1.5 line height and a fluid, two-line headline. Use 120–160 ms opacity/color transitions, respect reduced-motion preferences and suppress decorative motion. No autoplay. Use a single column at small widths and a two-column detail layout around 800 px. Put the purchase block immediately after the hero proof and summary in reading order.

| Area | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- |
| Security and operations | Validate Access token signature, issuer, audience, expiry and authorized owner on each admin read/write, including preview routes. Require provider MFA. Reject cross-origin mutations and use CSRF protection, bound lengths, parameterized queries and escaped output. Rate-limit public submissions, add a honeypot, reject binary uploads. Disable owner access on preview/development hostnames. Cache only public content. Keep credentials, buyer emails and signed URLs out of logs and source control. Test anonymous access, draft visibility, invalid forms, database export/restore and deployment rollback. [Access JWT verification](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/access-controls/applications/http-apps/authorization-cookie/validating-json/) (accessed 2026-11-26). |
| Performance | Server-render home/detail and reserve image dimensions. Responsive WebP or AVIF hero poster target ≤150 KB. Compress total first viewport transfer to ≤350 KB and initial JS to ≤50 KB compressed. Defer offscreen images. Load video only after play. Host previews on a domain controlled by the store. Test three uncached throttled mobile loads and one real mobile device, capture median LCP and CLS and test interaction latency. Target LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 at p75 by device. Label sparse field data as unavailable. [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) · [Cloudflare preview hosting](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/buckets/public-buckets/) (accessed 2026-11-26). |
| Accessibility | Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, correctly named controls, errors next to fields and announced status changes. Text contrast ≥4.5:1 (large text ≥3:1), meaningful UI boundaries ≥3:1. Aim for 44 × 44 px hit areas, reflow at 320 CSS px, test at 200% zoom, and read one complete purchase path with a screen reader. Provide equivalent scene descriptions and captions for any spoken content. Report hosted-checkout accessibility issues to the provider and offer human assistance. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) (accessed 2026-11-26). |
| SEO and sharing | Individual canonical URL, unique descriptive title and description, social image showing the actual character and human-readable HTML links for each published pack. Sitemap contains only live pages. Drafts return 404 to guests. Private pages disallow indexing and require authentication. Verify canonical/preview URLs after deployment and on actual shared links. |
| Privacy and recovery | Publish the seller's identity/contact, processors, purposes, retention and rights process from [10](10-risk-and-legal-assessment.md). Allow analytics on public merchandising pages and the generic feedback-submission confirmation only. Strip unknown query strings and do not submit form contents, contact details or buyer IDs as events. No receipt, checkout or account tokens in referrers, links or analytics. Handle mistyped email or disputes through native provider verification. |

**Assumption — instrumentation contract.** Use public page views on `/` and `/characters/*` for home and detail reach. Configure named goals `proof_play` (first genuine play), `checkout_click` (actual buy click), and `feedback_submit` (one message successfully saved). Count each action using the common page path/source dimensions. Identify SKU from the detail path. Use no custom properties or paid cross-domain attribution system. Separate **provider paid order/refund** counts from **self-reported usable shot** and delivery failures. An exit click, content page view or delivered email cannot establish a paid order, downloaded ZIP or successful generation. Record the channel as unknown when no approved campaign link or buyer report identifies it.

**Fact.** Plausible counts the same person on different days or devices as different visitors. Report unique visitors as this privacy-preserving proxy. Starter supports custom goals, and Gumroad's exported sale dates are UTC. Convert to Asia/Bangkok before reconciling the December cutoff. [Plausible metrics](https://plausible.io/docs/metrics-definitions) · [Custom goals](https://plausible.io/docs/custom-event-goals) · [Gumroad exports](https://gumroad.com/help/article/74-the-analytics-dashboard) (accessed 2026-11-26).
