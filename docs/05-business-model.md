## Decisions made

- **Assumption** — Sell one ZIP for one character. Launch with three nonhuman characters, one seller and one nonexclusive commercial license.
- **Assumption** — Set the standard price at US$29. Apply a US$10 discount automatically to the first ten paid pack units across the store. Buyer tax appears at checkout where applicable.
- **Assumption** — Use direct Gumroad links for hosted checkout, receipts, delivery and repeat downloads. Choose a weekly THB payout to the founder’s local bank.
- **Assumption** — Offer a 14-day money-back policy worldwide. Count paid orders, refunds and cleared bank deposits separately.

# Business model

All external sources in this file were accessed **November 26, 2026**. Dollar amounts are USD.

## What the buyer receives

| Component | Delivery promise and choice |
|---|---|
| Three separate PNG references | Front, side and three-quarter views of the same character. State dimensions, file sizes and ZIP version on the listing. Separate images can be uploaded directly. A collage alone would make the buyer crop their purchase. |
| Two copyable shot prompts and quick-start instructions | Name the exact model, mode, plan and test date. Show where to place the reference and what setting to use. Changes in appearance are possible. The buyer pays their own tool fees. |
| Proof before payment | Public two-shot demonstration from that character’s supplied references, with both prompts identifiable. The footage is a preview. It is outside the ZIP and the buyer’s asset license. |
| One license tier | One purchaser/operator may modify references, generate and monetize unlimited finished videos and deliver those finished videos to clients. Source PNGs, prompts and ZIP must stay with the buyer except for submission to permitted processing tools. Others can buy the same character. Full scope and rights checks are in [10-risk-and-legal-assessment.md](10-risk-and-legal-assessment.md). |

**Assumption** — Test individual character purchases. A bundle hides which character earned the sale. A recurring subscription obliges a solo seller to deliver a cadence that ten purchases cannot establish. Splitting personal and commercial prices would force buyers to make a rights decision before the core price has been tested.

## Price and willingness to pay

**Estimate** — At $19 the buyer risks less than a $20 custom concept quote, and the founder keeps about $14.80 after modeled card fees and a 5% risk allowance. At $29 the figure is $23.01. A $9 list price would leave about $6.59 under the same assumptions and push the shop toward volume before it has distribution. A $49 starting price would approach a quoted $50 custom turnaround, where a buyer can specify their own cast. These comparisons motivate a test. They establish no market-clearing price. [Comparable offers](04-competitor-analysis.md) · [Unit economics](06-revenue-and-financial-plan.md).

**Assumption** — $19 and $29 are offers to test. Interest, competing list prices and the founder’s production time do not establish what a stranger will pay. Present the same files, proof and license at both prices. Avoid a fictional previous price. Describe $29 as the standard price and the $19 offer as limited to ten pack units. [Evidence and comparable listings](04-competitor-analysis.md)

**Fact** — Gumroad can apply one fixed-amount discount automatically across selected products. Its quantity limit is shared and counts each eligible item. Set all three products to $29, disable multiple quantities and regional discounts, target the same three products with one $10 code capped at ten units and apply it automatically to each. Check the last discounted unit and an exhausted code with test products. Keep checkout authoritative and remove the introductory copy from the shop when the code is exhausted. [Discount settings](https://gumroad.com/help/article/128-discount-codes) · [Regional pricing settings](https://gumroad.com/help/article/327-purchasing-power-parity)

**Assumption** — Invite creators with an active recurring-character project to purchase an already deliverable pack during build week. Those transactions use the same offer and count toward the cap. Record unique visitors, price shown, checkout clicks, paid buyers and refund reasons. After ten introductory units, show $29 to the next cohort, with the same offer and channel wherever feasible. After 100 comparable $29 product-page visits, keep $29 if at least two distinct people pay. If zero pay and three independent prospects identify price after seeing the proof, interview each about what they bought instead and the actual price paid. Use that evidence to set a new price experiment at the month-end review. Keep the ten-pack cap intact. At one sale or materially different traffic, collect more observations. These are decision thresholds. The first ten orders are too few to justify splitting into A/B groups.

## Delivery, support and refunds

**Fact** — A Gumroad receipt opens the purchased content. A buyer can use the matching email for an optional Gumroad Library account. Upload the ZIP to Gumroad, use a separately compressed public preview on the shop and test a fresh buyer’s download, ZIP contents and receipt recovery. Use Gumroad’s required Terms field for the versioned buyer license. [Purchase access](https://gumroad.com/help/article/199-how-do-i-access-my-purchase) · [Checkout customization](https://gumroad.com/help/article/101-designing-your-product-page)

**Assumption** — Publish the following beside the buy button and in the receipt instructions. “Request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase for any reason. Reply to your receipt or contact Open Bestie with your order email or receipt number. If files are missing, damaged or materially different from the listing, contact us for a working replacement or the remedy you are entitled to. Your statutory rights still apply.” Respond within one business day. Refund through the original Gumroad order and log one optional reason. A full refund ends licensed use and hosted access. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Escalate to Gumroad if the available balance cannot fund a refund.

**Fact** — Gumroad may refund at its discretion within 90 days. It honors EU consumers’ 14-day withdrawal for website purchases and Brazilian consumers’ seven-day period from delivery, including on digital goods. On normal Gumroad-processed refunds, Gumroad returns its own fee and retains underlying processing costs.
