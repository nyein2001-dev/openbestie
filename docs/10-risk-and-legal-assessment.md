# Risk and legal assessment

This is a planning assessment, not legal advice. All external sources were accessed **November 26, 2026**. Inferences about this particular shop are labeled **Assumption**.

## Ownership and provenance

**Fact** — The U.S. Copyright Office’s 2025 report says copyright can protect human-created expression that remains perceptible in an AI-assisted work, including qualifying edits and selection or arrangement. Prompts alone generally do not confer sufficient control over the resulting image. Thailand’s Copyright Act defines an author as the person who makes or creates the work. The statute alone does not settle the copyright status of these particular AI-assisted characters. [U.S. Copyright Office findings](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html) · [Thai Copyright Act, sections 4, 6 and 8](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/3801)

**Assumption** — Prefer the founder’s own sketches, editable layers, original written prompts and manually corrected references. Save source artists, input origins/permissions, tool/plan/region/date, terms snapshot, payment proof, edit history, final ZIP hash and public proof frames. Verify separately the rights to **redistribute editable standalone files**, to authorize ordinary buyer use, to allow the resale platform to market/deliver them, and to allow the tested video provider to process and reuse inputs on its terms. A license for finished ads alone fails the first test. Exclude stock textures, preset characters, trademarked props, third-party voices and music unless every required right is documented. If an upstream art generator was used, inspect that exact account’s terms before listing. The founder’s generator is unspecified.

**Fact** — Gumroad requires authority to grant resale and distribution rights and makes the supplier responsible for end-user license terms. A provider’s allocation of output rights cannot settle third-party authorship or infringement. [Gumroad supplier agreement, sections 6.5–6.9](https://gumroad.com/terms) · [Copyright Office report](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html)

## Similarity, complaints and piracy

**Fact** — WIPO’s Global Brand Database supports mark searches and recommends checking relevant national/regional registers. U.S. digital-replica protections vary, and copyright alone cannot establish a person’s consent to use their likeness. EU AI Act Article 50 addresses transparency for certain generated content, including deepfakes. Scope depends on the content and role. [WIPO search guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb) · [U.S. Copyright Office digital replicas study](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-1-Digital-Replicas-Report.pdf) · [European Commission Article 50 guidance](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50?full=1)

**Assumption** — Search Open Bestie, each character name, look and distinguishing symbols against the web, relevant trademark registers and reverse-image results. Compare silhouettes, costumes and color combinations against recognizable characters. Save dates/results. Label AI-generated proof, omit real people and do not imply endorsement. A visually distinctive nonhuman design can still resemble a protected mascot. Redesign or get a legal review if a match is credible.

**Assumption** — Publish a rights-contact form or email. Ask for the listing URL, claimed work/right, contact and supporting material. Acknowledge within one business day. For a credible claim, suspend the listing and hosted sales, preserve records and alert the reseller. Check the full claim, seek counsel for disputed ownership or formal notices, and inform affected buyers of replacement or refund options. Restore access only on a documented resolution. For copied packs elsewhere, retain purchase/provenance evidence and use the host’s reporting process only for rights the founder controls. Use limited-resolution previews and private paid-file access. Strong DRM introduces friction and cannot recover redistributed ZIPs. [Gumroad reporting channel](https://gumroad.com/help/article/286-how-do-i-report-a-gumroad-creator) · [Gumroad terms](https://gumroad.com/terms)

## Consumer rights, Thai registration and tax

**Fact** — The EU’s ordinary withdrawal period for distance sales is 14 days. Its digital-content exception has conditions including prior express consent, acknowledgment and confirmation. Defective digital content also has statutory remedies. Gumroad currently honors EU 14-day withdrawals for website purchases. Offer the 14 days worldwide and honor longer applicable remedies. Check the MoR’s withdrawal process, including the online withdrawal function where applicable, and make the support route visible. [Consumer Rights Directive, arts. 9, 11a and 16(m)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02011L0083-20260927) · [Digital Content Directive, arts. 13–14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj) · [Gumroad refund policy](https://gumroad.com/help/article/51-what-is-gumroads-refund-policy)

**Fact** — Gumroad acts as customer-facing reseller and handles applicable buyer sales taxes, VAT/GST and invoicing on its transactions. A Thai individual may still owe personal income tax. The Revenue Department generally requires VAT registration for a person regularly supplying taxable goods/services in Thailand when annual turnover exceeds ฿1.8 million, typically within 30 days of reaching the threshold. The export-of-services zero rate has conditions. Foreign buyers alone do not prove eligibility. Government guidance calls for commercial registration within 30 days of starting covered online trading. [Gumroad agreement, section 6](https://gumroad.com/terms) · [Thai personal income tax](https://www.rd.go.th/english/6045.html) · [Thai VAT](https://www.rd.go.th/english/6043.html) · [Thai e-commerce registration](https://thailand.go.th/public/useful-information-detail/006_104)

**Assumption** — On Day 1, check the founder’s legal right to operate, trade-registration treatment and applicable local filing office for the single-supplier/MoR arrangement. Ask a Thai accountant about income classification, supplier-to-reseller VAT, turnover across the founder’s activities, documented FX method, reporting dates, allowable expenses and any withholding. Retain reseller terms, orders, refunds, fee records, payout statements and bank transfers. Do not book buyer taxes as product sales. [Payouts and economics](05-business-model.md)

## Privacy and incidents

**Fact** — Thailand’s PDPA addresses transparency, lawful bases, safeguards, overseas transfers, data-subject rights and breach notification. GDPR can cover a non-EU seller offering goods/services to people in the EU or monitoring their behavior there. Mere web accessibility alone does not establish targeting. Both frameworks impose conditional breach-notification duties with a 72-hour regulatory time frame where feasible and different individual-notice thresholds. [Thai PDPA, especially §§23–24, 28–29, 30–37](https://mdes.go.th/law/detail/1909-Personal-Data-Protection-Act--B-E--2562--2019-) · [GDPR, arts. 3, 5–6, 13, 27–28, 32–34 and 44–49](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/) · [EDPB on territorial scope](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_3_2018_territorial_scope_after_public_consultation_en_1.pdf)

**Assumption** — Publish the founder’s controller identity/contact, purpose, lawful basis, fields collected, vendor recipients/countries, retention periods and rights/complaint process. Use contract necessity for fulfillment and requested support where applicable, and legal obligation for required tax records. Assess and document the basis for minimal operational analytics. Separate optional follow-up and promotional consent. Do not put personal data, full query strings or order tokens into analytics. Use aggregate event counts and structured feedback over session recordings. Reconcile provider privacy roles, appropriate vendor agreements and transfer safeguards before sending buyer data abroad. Counsel should check EU targeting, overseas representatives where required and any country-specific consent rules.

**Assumption** — Restrict admin access to the founder with MFA. Keep payment-card details in hosted checkout. Review feedback monthly and erase identifiable research notes after 90 days unless the participant expressly agrees to further contact. Keep finance/dispute records only for the applicable statutory/evidentiary period confirmed by the accountant. Honor access, correction and deletion requests subject to retention duties. On an incident, contain it, log when it became known, assess risk and applicable regulators, and promptly notify the authority and affected people where required. Coordinate with Gumroad without assuming it handles the shop’s independent obligations. [Gumroad privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy)
