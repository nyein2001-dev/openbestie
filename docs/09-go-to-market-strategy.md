## Decisions made

- **Assumption.** Approach creators with a recent multi-shot short and an upcoming role they can cast. Lead with the finished two-shot example for that role.
- **Estimate.** Spend at most 24 acquisition hours from the December pilot through Dec 30. Allocate four hours to creator partners, six to direct conversations, eight to existing communities and six to owned demos. Budget $0 for media.
- **Assumption.** Offer the first ten paid packs collectively at $19, then charge $29. Give every buyer the same nonexclusive commercial license and 14-calendar-day refund policy.
- **Estimate.** Four channels must deliver 500 measured storefront visitors and ten distinct paying buyers. Reallocate time weekly using actual orders per hour.

# Go-to-market strategy

### Buyer, message and conversion asset

**Assumption — ideal customer.** An English-speaking solo creator has published a short that cuts between shots, uses or can access the tested Kling workflow, and has editorial control over a new recurring character. Qualify with a link to a recent piece and a specific upcoming scene. A buyer who needs the same unique face as an existing series, an exclusive brand mascot or a commission should hear the restriction before checkout.

**Assumption — launch message.** “Need the same character for your close-up and your wide shot? Watch both. This pack includes the three references and two prompts used in the demo. $29, or $19 while the first ten pack discounts last. You supply Kling access and credits.” Link directly to that character's demonstration. Tailor the first line to the creator's project. Do not claim guaranteed consistency, money saved, customer results, exclusivity or affiliation.

**Fact.** Real creators share workflows publicly, Kling operates a discoverable creator Discord, and Google directs Flow creators to its Discord channel. Public presence identifies places to listen. Whether their moderators permit links is a separate question. Check local rules and use a designated showcase or opt-in invitation. ### Funnel and attribution

| Estimated step | Calculation | What to do if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 500 measured storefront visitors → 250 product visitors | 50% reach a character detail page, including direct landings. | If a home visitor cannot explain what is sold, replace the copy or above-fold proof. If partner reach fails, direct those hours to conversations with creators who have active projects. |
| 250 detail visitors → 40 checkout clickers | 16% click a pack's Buy action. | Observe one prospect selecting a pack. Inspect character fit, proof, price, tool access and license objections. |
| 40 checkout clickers → 10 retained buyers | 25% complete a paid order that remains valid at close. Blended storefront conversion is 2%. | Inspect merchant availability, discount, taxes and price on phone. Ask an opted-in nonbuyer where they stopped. |
| 10 buyers → $190 gross | Ten independent $19 purchases, one per buyer, no refunds in the base case. | Report actual customers, units, realized price and refunds independently. One person buying multiple packs uses multiple discount units. |

**Assumption — counts.** Use tagged storefront URLs for the four channels. Use daily unique goal counts and treat cross-page totals as approximate. Deduplicate paid customers in the private order export. Assign a sale to one known source using the buyer's optional answer or verified referral evidence. Leave ambiguous sales “unknown” and still include them in total revenue. Clicks and checkout starts never earn revenue credit. Native Gumroad UTM links have their own attribution windows if used. ## Assumptions & open questions

- Will two independent partners send 150 qualified visitors without payment? Ten unanswered invitations invalidate that reach assumption for December.
- Does proof of one distinct nonhuman character attract the next episode's buyer? If qualified prospects consistently need a pre-existing character, apply the stop rule in [02](02-problem-validation.md).
- Can 24 focused acquisition hours produce 500 measured visitors with modest overlap? Record actual effort, unique visitors, unknown attribution and sales every week.
