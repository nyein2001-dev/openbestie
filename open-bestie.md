# Open Bestie

> **Ready-to-use characters for your AI videos. Find a character. Make it yours. Bring it to life.**

## Project Overview

**Open Bestie** is a marketplace for ready-to-use digital characters
designed for AI video creators. It helps creators discover, acquire, and
use distinctive characters in AI video generation tools such as Kling,
Google Flow, Seedance, and other compatible platforms.

The platform brings together reusable character assets in one place,
making it easier for creators to find the right character for their
videos without having to design and prepare every character from
scratch.

## Vision

Make it easy for anyone to find a character they love and bring that
character to life through AI-generated video.

## Mission

Provide a curated marketplace of high-quality, production-ready digital
characters that creators can use across AI video-generation workflows.

## What Open Bestie Offers

OpenBestie focuses on ready-to-use character assets, including:

-   **Animal Characters** --- expressive animals and mascot-style
    characters.
-   **Robotic Pets** --- robot companions, mechanical animals, and
    futuristic pets.
-   **Fantasy Creatures** --- mythical beings, monsters, and imaginative
    creatures.
-   **Cinematic Characters** --- distinctive characters designed for
    cinematic storytelling.
-   **Animated Mascots** --- memorable mascots for content, campaigns,
    and creative projects.
-   **Hybrid Creatures** --- original characters that combine traits from
    animals, robots, or fantasy creatures, sometimes with stylized
    human features.

### Proposed Character Pack

-   **Character reference sheet** with front, side, and back views and a
    set of facial expressions.
-   **Platform-specific prompts** tested in the workflows listed on the
    product page, with setup notes where needed.
-   **Sample video** showing the character in motion and identifying the
    tool and workflow used.
-   **Usage license** covering permitted uses, any commercial-use terms,
    and restrictions on sharing or resale.

Each listing should identify the included files, formats, and tested
workflows. Describe compatibility in terms of what was tested. Confirm
any platform integration or endorsement before mentioning it.

## Target Customers (Proposed)

-   AI video creators and short-form content teams, including YouTubers, TikTok creators, and social media content teams.
-   Independent filmmakers, digital storytellers, and animation creators
    making short films or recurring series.
-   Small businesses, marketers, and agencies looking for a mascot for
    campaigns or branded content.

## Core Value Proposition

Open Bestie gives creators a head start on character design. They can
see how a character looks and moves before purchasing, then use the
reference sheet and tested prompts to help preserve its visual identity
across scenes.

## Brand Positioning

Open Bestie should feel:

-   **Friendly and approachable** --- a welcoming place to discover
    creative characters.
-   **Playful and expressive** --- inspired by internet culture and
    creator communities.
-   **Creative and modern** --- built for the emerging AI-native content
    ecosystem.
-   **Trustworthy and practical** --- clear about asset contents, usage
    rights, and compatibility.

The name **Open Bestie** suggests a place to meet a new character for
your next project. The site should keep that friendly tone while making
it clear that buyers get downloadable character assets under a license.

### Proposed Collections

| Collection | Characters |
| --- | --- |
| **Besties** | Pets, mascots, and endearing creatures |
| **Frenemies** | Rivals, opponents, and foils |

## Brand Voice and Product Language

Use a friendly, playful, Gen Z voice in discovery and character pages.
Keep prices, checkout, license terms, and download actions explicit.

| Conventional marketplace term | Open Bestie language |
| --- | --- |
| Shop / Browse characters | **Meet the Besties** |
| Character listing | **Bestie** |
| Buy | **Adopt** / **Unlock** |
| Add to cart | **Add to squad** |
| My purchases | **My Squad** |
| Product details | **Get to know** |
| Download files | **Download character pack** |

Use **Adopt** for pets, mascots, and characters that fit the language of
adoption. Use **Unlock** as the general purchase label, especially for
rivals and villains. Show the price and license terms alongside either
purchase action.

## Important Product Principles

1.  **Ready to use:** Character assets should be prepared for practical
    use in supported AI video workflows.
2.  **Consistency:** Provide the references and instructions needed to
    help creators maintain a character's appearance across scenes.
3.  **Transparency:** Clearly describe included files, supported
    formats, limitations, licensing, and usage rights.
4.  **Platform flexibility:** Test and document each supported AI video
    workflow individually, including any tool-specific setup or limits.
5.  **Originality and responsible use:** Review rights to character art,
    reference materials, and likenesses. Obtain appropriate consent and
    rights for any use of a real person's likeness and follow applicable
    platform policies.

## Long-Term Direction

| Stage | Catalog direction |
| --- | --- |
| Launch | Begin with animals, robotic pets, fantasy creatures, stylized cinematic characters, mascots, and hybrids. Human-like virtual models are outside the launch scope. |
| Catalog expansion | Add more rivals, opponents, and darker characters through **Frenemies** and **Villain Era**. |
| Later exploration | Consider human-like virtual models after reviewing likeness rights, consent, platform policies, and misuse risks. |

The long-term goal is to become the first place creators look when
they need a distinctive character for their next AI video.
